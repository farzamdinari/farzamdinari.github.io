This conference paper investigates the prediction of extra-urban road accident rates across ten Iranian provinces using artificial intelligence and transportation-related factors.

The study uses data collected from the statistical yearbooks of the Iranian Road Maintenance and Transportation Organization for the period from 2021 to 2023. A Multilayer Perceptron Artificial Neural Network was developed to identify the factors that have the greatest influence on extra-urban accident rates.

The proposed MLP model achieved a prediction accuracy of **85.3%**. The results indicate that road-maintenance facilities, traffic-safety infrastructure, technical inspection capacity, and road-management indicators play important roles in predicting and potentially reducing accident rates.

### Abstract

Road accidents represent a major social and public-safety challenge. This research aims to predict extra-urban accident rates in ten Iranian provinces and identify the most influential factors that may contribute to reducing them.

A Multilayer Perceptron neural network was applied to accident, infrastructure, road-maintenance, and transportation data collected from 2021 to 2023. The results demonstrated an accident-prediction accuracy of 85.3% and identified several important road-management and infrastructure indicators.

The findings may support transportation planners and decision-makers in identifying high-risk areas, improving road infrastructure, and developing preventive traffic-safety policies.
