Engineering Economics introduces economic methods for evaluating engineering alternatives and making informed investment decisions. The course connects engineering analysis with topics such as cash flows, interest rates, present and future worth, project comparison, and economic decision-making. I had the pleasure of working with Prof. Maryam Rezapour Niari as a teaching assistant.

Here are some of the main parts of my teaching experience:

### Designing weekly practice questions

My main responsibility was designing weekly practice questions that allowed students to apply the concepts discussed in class. Preparing these exercises required presenting economic decision problems clearly and selecting appropriate numerical examples.

### Connection to decision-making

What makes Engineering Economics especially relevant to my interests is its emphasis on choosing among alternatives under financial and operational constraints. Its methods are closely connected to project evaluation, industrial decision-making, and resource allocation.

### What I did (virtually):
* Designed weekly practice questions
* Supported students’ understanding of economic evaluation methods
